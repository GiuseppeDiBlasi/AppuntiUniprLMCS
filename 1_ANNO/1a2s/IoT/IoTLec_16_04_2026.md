- Smart cities

# Main issues in IoT security

In the past most attacks concerned traditional computer systems and networks.
Most of the security mechanisms and protocols has been developed for standard internet-connected systems

IoT bring new security challenges:
- In IoT we have a highly heterogenous devices (sensors, actuators and so on)
- Devices may suffer from their own vulnerabilities, especcialy in case of very constrained devices (limited computational power, memory, communication resources and the lack of a UI)
- They use different communication protocols, sometimes proprietary protocols
- Application may be not well tested
- Difficulty to apply patches and updates which impacts the software life cycle
- Presence of different attack surfaces and vectors

# Attack domains

- Physical and Hardware: related to direct access to the system, may consist in the replecement, destruction, modification or explotation of hardware components

- Communications: explotation of possible vulnerabilities of communication techonoglies, both wired and wireless, and protocols at different layers (data-link, transport, network/routing)

- Software: type of attack that tries to exploit possible application weaknesses in design or in the implementation

# OWASP IoT TOP 10 vulnerabilities 

1) Weak Guessable, or Hardcoded passwords: 
    - Easy to bruteforce
    - Publicly available (defpass.com)
    - Unchanged credentials (or unchangable)

2) Insecure Network Sevices:
    - unneeded or insecure network services running on the device
    - may be connected to the internet -> leads to unathorized access

3) Insecure Ecosystem Interfaces:
    - Insecure interfaces in the ecosystem outside the device (Web, Backend API, cloud or mobile interfaces)
    - Common issues: lack or waek authetication, authorization, encryption and so on

4) Lack of secure update mechanism:
    - lack of ability to securely update the device
    - lack of firmware
    - lack of anti-rollback mechanism or notification security changes due to updates

5), until 10) "lightly skipped", don't know if they are really that important

# Possible countermeasures

- Against physical threats:
    - security policies
    - phyisically protect the system/device

- Against communication threats:
    - security policies on services and protocols
    - secure communication protocols and cryptographic algorithm

- Against software threats:
    - use well-tested and updated software
    - security policies in all software life-cycle

SigFox vs LoRaWAN:
- Sigfox:
    - Fully vertically integrated in the company
    - At the moment there is no development since the proprietary comapny went bankrupt
    - Closed ecosystem

- LoRaWAN: 
    - Open specification by the LoRa Alliance
    - Semtechs provides the radio chips, but the ecosystem is open (multi-vendor)
    - Strong application in research and private deployments

Main difference is control vs opennes of the ecosystems

LPWA is suited for application such as Smart Parking, Alarms for smart cities, agricultural assets

If we want to send a few bytes occasionaly and have a battery life in the order of years, then it's convinient to use LoRa.


The physical layer of LoRa is actually proprietary, its based on the CSS (Chirp Spread Spectrum) modulation. The MAC layer is actually open with a access method based on ALOHA and it's not IP-compliant.
The duty cycle is 1% without channel access method.
